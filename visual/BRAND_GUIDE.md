# Bunny OS brand guide

## Brand promise

Bunny Desktop is calm enough to disappear during focused work and precise
enough to make system state, authority, and consequence unmistakable. Its
personality is intelligent, spatial, soft, trustworthy, and future-facing.

## The Bunny mark

The mark is built from two rising open arcs mirrored around a quiet central
axis. The paired vertical curves suggest attention and direction without using
a literal rabbit illustration. Clear space around the mark is at least one
quarter of its height.

Use the mark deliberately at boot, login, launcher, About, selected empty
states, and within wallpaper composition. Do not use it as list bullets,
decorative confetti, a loading spinner, or a replacement for standard system
symbols.

The symbolic mark is one color and inherits the current foreground. The full
mark may pair Bunny Violet with Bunny Sky only at identity scale. Never stretch,
rotate, outline, add facial features, apply glow, or place the mark on a
low-contrast field.

## Voice

Use direct, composed language:

- State observed facts before interpretations.
- Name the action and consequence on buttons: `Restart to update`, not `OK`.
- Say `Unavailable` when state is not observed; do not say `Safe` or `Complete`.
- Use `Bunny` for the assistant only when the surface is clearly assistant
  context. Use `Bunny OS` for the operating system.

## Color

The signature pair is Bunny Violet and Bunny Sky. Violet is the primary accent;
Sky is reserved for focus, active privacy, information, and spatial highlights.
Success, warning, and danger are semantic and always accompanied by an icon and
text. Dark and light themes use independently tuned semantic values rather than
mechanical inversion.

## Imagery

Wallpapers use atmospheric depth, paired arcs, directional horizon lines, and
generous negative space. They must be original, abstract, quiet, and useful
behind windows. Avoid characters, photographic subjects, dense textures,
branded mascots, neon grids, and continuous animated effects.

## Naming

Product surfaces use title case: `Bunny Command Palette`, `Approval Center`,
`FocusMode`, and `CompactLayout`. Status phrases use sentence case.
