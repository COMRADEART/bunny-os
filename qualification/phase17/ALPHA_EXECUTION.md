# Alpha execution

Alpha reports enter through Phase 9 and remain governed by Phase 12 triage,
deduplication, reproduction, and report semantics. User reports, measurements,
reproductions, hardware/accessibility/performance/security observations, and
unbound useful evidence retain their distinct classes. The Phase 13 owner
policy decides sufficiency thresholds. With no active applicable policy,
zero or one hundred reports both derive `SUFFICIENCY_UNDETERMINED`.
