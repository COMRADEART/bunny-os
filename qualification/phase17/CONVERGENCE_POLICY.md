# Convergence and conflict policy

Security reconciliation, dimensional hardware qualification, cryptographic
signing verification, independent ordered approval, and policy-based Alpha
sufficiency retain their own meanings. Phase 17 asks the owning engines and
converges their results; it does not reinterpret five JSON booleans.

All five rows must contribute. Zero through four can never authorize. A five
of five fixture universe may demonstrate the mechanical path only in scratch,
and Phase 13 must still validate an external release-authority record.

Unresolved same-source or cross-source conflict derives
`REQUIRES_HUMAN_DECISION` or the existing more-blocking state. Votes are not
counted, scores are not averaged, recency does not favor a pleasant answer,
and no evidence class automatically outranks another.
