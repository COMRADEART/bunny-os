# Shell tour

1. Select Bunny at GDM and confirm Wayland with `loginctl show-session "$XDG_SESSION_ID" -p Type`.
2. Inspect the Bunny panel menu without treating its unknown status as secure.
3. Open GNOME overview, quick settings, lock, Files, Settings, and normal Terminal.
4. Open Bunny Launcher, Workspaces, Tasks, Approvals, and Privacy.
5. Stop Bunny Core and broker separately; verify the documented degraded behavior.
6. Log out cleanly.
