# Three-minute demo

1. Boot/select Bunny and show the Bunny panel plus normal GNOME controls.
2. Super+Space: launch Terminal, then route `Check for system updates` to confirmation.
3. Open a project workspace and show that its files are untouched.
4. Show a read-only and destructive terminal proposal; neither executes.
5. Open Privacy and show telemetry disabled/local-only state.
6. Mention the signed Bunny placeholder and show honest unavailable mode.
7. Log out and point to Bunny (Safe Shell).
