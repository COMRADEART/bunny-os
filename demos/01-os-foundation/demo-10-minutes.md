# Ten-minute demo

1. Show exact image build invocation, completed log, provenance, checksum and SBOM.
2. Boot the checked QCOW2 in the recorded QEMU/KVM tuple.
3. Complete first login and show privacy/offline choices.
4. Launch verified Bunny Desktop; with the current placeholder, show and label the safe refusal instead.
5. Show capability discovery through `bunny-os-info --json` and contract 1.0.0/protocol 3.
6. Request safe system status through the broker.
7. Show an unauthorized/unknown broker request denied and its redacted audit row.
8. Inspect update status and signed manifest checks on the disposable test channel.
9. Stage a real digest-pinned deployment, or show the unit-test simulation labelled simulation.
10. List/select the real previous deployment and show rollback-attack rejection.
11. Schedule and boot the Bunny-independent recovery target.
12. Export diagnostics, inspect permissions/redaction, then return to normal boot.

The presenter records each row pass/fail. Placeholder, simulated update, or unbooted recovery cannot be reported as the corresponding runtime pass.
